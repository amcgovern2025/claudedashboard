Research Foundation

This analysis framework is informed by Dr. Lisa Mosconi's body of research at the Weill Cornell Women's Brain Initiative. Key research areas include:

Brain Metabolism

Reduced cerebral glucose metabolism as an early Alzheimer's biomarker, often preceding structural atrophy by years.

Structural MRI Biomarkers

Gray matter volume, white matter integrity, and hippocampal measurements as indicators of brain health and dementia risk.

Regional Vulnerability

Focus on hippocampus, posterior cingulate cortex, medial temporal lobe, and entorhinal cortex as early-affected regions.

Sex Differences & Hormones

Menopause-related changes in brain structure, connectivity, and energy metabolism as an Alzheimer's risk window.

Nutrition & Lifestyle

Mediterranean diet associations with reduced brain atrophy and lower amyloid biomarker burden.

Familial Risk

Increased fibrillar amyloid-β burden in individuals with family history of late-onset Alzheimer's disease.
